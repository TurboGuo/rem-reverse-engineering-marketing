# Disclaimer

REM reads **public** social media posts to study marketing patterns: how often an account posts, when, about what, and how each post is structured.

* Use it to learn structure, timing and strategy. Do not copy someone's words, videos or images, and do not present their work as your own.
* Follow the terms of service of each platform and of the data provider (Monid).
* Do not use REM to track, profile or harass private individuals. Point it at public brands and public figures acting in a public, professional role.
* Numbers come from the data provider at the time of the pull and can change. Some conclusions are inferences (for example, likely paid reach); the page labels them as such.
* REM does not watch videos. Video structure is read from captions, titles and descriptions.

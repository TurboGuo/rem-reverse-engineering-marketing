REM: Higgsfield Social Playbook

Higgsfield runs social like a launch desk. Almost every day it ships one product or partner model, cuts one hero video, and puts it on X, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn within the same hour. Each platform gets its own call to action. TikTok is left to creators, who did more for the brand there than the official account ever did.

The eight things they post about

Over the month, every official post fell into one of these buckets. The first three carried most of the reach.

1 · Biggest reach

Own product launch

"Introducing Higgsfield Katana", "AI Influencer", "Genjutsu Lite", "Academy". A bold first line, three or four ">" feature bullets, then "Available now on Higgsfield."

X average 2.32M views across 5 posts. The top post, "Officially, every viral AI influencer was made on Higgsfield", reached 5.08M.

2 · Borrowed audience

Partner model on day one

As soon as Kling, FLUX, Wan, Nano Banana, Ideogram or ElevenLabs ship a model, Higgsfield announces it is "live on Higgsfield", often with a few days of exclusive access.

X average 1.02M views across 4 posts. "Kling is back" alone reached 3.67M.

3 · Ride the giants

Claude and ChatGPT experiments

"We gave Claude Opus 5.5 access to 12 laptops." "We asked Claude how good it is at motion design." These frame Higgsfield as the tool AI agents reach for.

Integration and experiment posts averaged 0.82M to 0.88M views on X. On Instagram, the Opus 5.5 motion design reel was the top post of the month with 56,972 likes.

4

Money on the table

A $20M API cashback pool, credit resets, "5 free generations", "unlimited for 7 days", "50% off until Oct 31". There is almost always a deadline.

"100% cashback on every model" reached 1.10M views on X.

5

Proof and open source breakdowns

"Passport Rush" and "ANERNEQ" were published with every prompt and asset made public. Other posts make a claim like "No video AI model was used to make this video."

Passport Rush drew 1,358 bookmarks on X, a high save rate for a post with 432K views.

6

Community programs

The $1,000,000 Global Film Festival (67,223 entries, 126 shortlisted), the Creator Partnership Program and creator credits like "Credits: @JeanPhilMadame".

These feed the creator posts that now carry TikTok.

7

Company milestones

The $1B revenue run rate, new investors (NVIDIA, TCV, S4S, Iman Gadzhi), a LinkedIn Top Startups listing, G2 badges, the opening in Seoul.

On LinkedIn these beat product posts: the $1B post got 780 likes and 134 shares.

8

Education and side hustles

Higgsfield Academy, director style prompting, "5 AI Side Hustles", "Two Clients = $1,000 a Month Clipping". This is beginner content that turns viewers into users.

This bucket holds most of the YouTube long videos and Shorts.

Recipe

The Comment KEYWORD engine

Each reel asks for one word in all caps that matches the product: OPUS, VIRAL, KATANA, API, TAXI, BANANA, ACADEMY. An automated DM sends the link back. Every comment tells the algorithm the reel is worth showing, so a link request also works as reach.

  • Average comments with a keyword: 2,947 per reel (44 reels). Without one: 1,809 per reel, but that is only 3 reels, so treat the gap as a hint, not proof.
  • On some reels comments outnumber likes. "Higgsfield × GPT Sol 6.1 for ads" got 11,422 comments on 15,115 likes. The credit reset post got 11,173 comments on 7,389 likes.
  • Instagram gets more posts than X does. Tutorials ("How to change any character in your video") and episodes of an AI influencer drama series appear only here.

The caption recipe

[Hook line, same as the X post] Comment "KEYWORD" to get the link 🔗 [One line of value] > Point 1 > Point 2 > Point 3 Available now on <brand site>. [Reel: the same hero video, recut]

Keyword DM automation needs a tool like ManyChat. Higgsfield's tool isn't visible in the data; only the "Comment" pattern is.

Recipe

What YouTube is for

X and Instagram announce. YouTube teaches. Long videos are full workflows ("How To Prompt Like a Director", "How To Animate a Short Film with Blender + Higgsfield") and money angles ("5 AI Side Hustles", "Clipping The Easiest Side Hustle"). Every description gives away the prompts in exchange for a click to the site.

  • The Shorts are a separate show. Each one is a single tip or claim aimed at beginners: "Stop Reshooting. AI Can Change Your Camera Angle", "How Much Clippers Get Paid for 100K Views".
  • Launch videos get recut into Shorts. The Katana Short was the most viewed Short at 214,379, about 10 times the Shorts average.
  • Guests add credibility, for example "Can AI Beat VFX? (feat. ErikDoesVFX)".

Recipe

The brand doesn't post here. Its users do.

Higgsfield runs TikTok through its users instead of an account. The Creator Partnership Program, announced on X on Oct 1, pays creators in credits and affiliate earnings and gives them early access. Creators caption their videos "Made with Higgsfield" and add the tag. The top creator video, a parrot dance school by @kennyslowbird, reached 10.77M views. That is more than double the best official X post (5.08M).

  • The features that spread on TikTok are the ones that put a face or a pet into a trend: Genjutsu and AI Influencer.
  • Creators are global. The top 19 came from Brazil, Kazakhstan, Pakistan, Japan, Korea, Ukraine and the US.
  • When copying this, sell features that make a good video by themselves, then pay creators to post them. An official TikTok account isn't required.

Copy it: a scaled week for a small team

Higgsfield has a full content team. This week keeps their structure but sizes it for one or two people. It is my adaptation of their pattern, not something taken from their data.

Checklist before every launch post

  • The hook works without the video: a claim, "Introducing", or "We gave X to Y".
  • Three ">" bullets, each with a number or a concrete verb.
  • X: no link in the main post. Put it in your own reply.
  • Instagram: a "Comment KEYWORD" line and DM automation turned on.
  • YouTube: a tracked link to "All prompts and assets".
  • LinkedIn: rewritten for a team and its metrics, with proof added.
  • A deadline or free tier ("5 free", "7 days", "until Oct 31").
  • Credit the creator by handle if the work came from them.

What not to copy

  • Their volume. 150+ official posts a month takes a team. Start with the Monday launch and the daily Shorts.
  • Large cashback pools need real budget. A small "first 50 users" offer can create the same urgency.
  • An idle official account. Higgsfield can leave TikTok to creators because it has a paid creator program. If you don't have one, post there yourself.

What he posts about

1 · Biggest reach

Copy my AI workflow

He shows a workflow he really uses and pastes the full prompts. "I'm GrokBot pilled. My 4 favorite routines (copy/paste these prompts)." The wardrobe app post went out the same minute on X, Threads and LinkedIn.

GrokBot post: 657,263 views, 2,610 likes, 3,162 bookmarks. 3,162 ÷ 2,610 = 1.21 bookmarks per like, which means people saved it to use later.

2 · Revenue

Paid launch explainers

Big launches from OpenAI (dots), Microsoft (Copilot) and DoorDash, written as "I've had early access" explainers and tagged #ad or #partner. 6 posts across LinkedIn and TikTok.

The OpenAI dots TikTok reached 11,438,770 views, against a median of 4,416 for his other TikToks.

3 · Daily volume

AI for science and robotics

One TikTok each weekday: a scorpion antivenom designed with Claude, brain scans turned into images, a laundry robot, nuclear fusion. They open with an emoji and one surprising sentence.

17 videos, 212,252 views in total. The best was Brain IT at 86,031.

4

AI model news

Odd moments from the frontier labs: a GPT 6 Astra model playing Minecraft for 141 hours, a Claude model swearing at a CAPTCHA.

2 TikToks, 18,962 and 2,205 views.

5

Founder life

The first team retreat in Lisbon, a "q3 in the books" photo dump. These are personal and show the company behind the newsletter.

The retreat post was his top LinkedIn post of the month with 147 likes.

6

One line takes, tested in replies

On Oct 13 he replied to another founder: "the only moat is the ability to move faster than the pace of change". Two days later he posted the same line on its own.

The standalone version drew 111 replies on 27,747 views.

7

Warm replies to builders

Short praise to founders he covers or interviews: "You guys move FAST" to Brett Adcock, "You guys crushed it" to Chris Park. This builds the relationships that bring exclusive interviews.

5 replies in the month, averaging 3,757 views each.

8

Everything funnels to The Rundown

Workflows link to The Rundown's Community Workflow Hub. Bios say "Join 2,000,000+ Readers". The personal brand works as a front door to the newsletter.

X bio location field: "Join 2,000,000+ Readers →".

Copy it: a week for a solo creator

This is my adaptation of his pattern for one person, not something taken from his data.

Launch day

Checklist for a workflow post

  • It is something you really use, written in first person.
  • The full prompt is in the post, ready to paste.
  • A number in the hook: "My 4 favorite routines".
  • Same minute on X, Threads and LinkedIn, rewritten for LinkedIn as a story.
  • A reply with the link to your own site, not in the main post.

What not to copy

  • Posting this rarely on X only works with 600K followers. A new account needs daily posts to grow, the way he did in 2023.
  • Paid partner posts come after the audience exists. Always tag them #ad.
  • Don't post the same video to every platform. He keeps TikTok separate from X.
